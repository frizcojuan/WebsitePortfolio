Cycle Count Tool Website

PT DSV Solutions Indonesia (Internship Final Project) • August - December 2025

HTML/CSS JavaScript PHP Warehouse Operations WMS

Overview

The Cycle Count Tool Website was developed as the final internship project during my tenure as a Warehouse Administration Staff at PT DSV Solutions Indonesia. The system is designed to digitalize and streamline the inventory cycle counting process, helping the warehouse team verify physical stock against WMS data faster and more accurately.

Key Features & Problem Solutions

  • Digital Inventory Verification: Enables real-time entry and validation of stock physical counts to reduce human error during manual paper-based logging.
  • Automated Discrepancy Detection: Instantly compares physical count data against expected WMS records to highlight discrepancies for rapid investigation.
  • Cycle Count Scheduling & Logs: Organizes daily cycle counting routines, helping staff monitor progress across various warehouse zones efficiently.
  • Data Export & Reporting: Generates structured summaries compatible with Microsoft Excel for administrative record-keeping and audit purposes.

Project Impact

By transitioning to this web-based tool, operational delays in identifying inventory variances were reduced significantly. The tool directly supports Kaizen continuous improvement initiatives within warehouse administration, resulting in notable time and cost savings.
